Controls
Palette: click, then clickpick up a palette item, click the map to put it down (or drag it there)
Click a pieceselects it; press and drag to move it
Drag a pieceswings it on its bridge to the earlier piece, keeping the length
Shift+dragmoves the whole set snapped together (Ctrl+drag too)
Alt+dragpulls the piece off its bridges
Double-click / double-tapunsnaps a piece from every bridge (a bridge: its far end)
R / Shift+Rrotates a free junction or curve 15° (side panel: 15° / 45°)
Tabjunction on one bridge: next port · while placing: another port
Fflips a curve on its bridge
D8 directions on / off (bridges every 45°)
Ctrl+C / Ctrl+Xcopy / cut the selected piece with its loose bridges (with Shift: its whole snapped set)
Ctrl+Vpaste on the pointer: click to drop, R turns it, Esc cancels
Deldeletes the selection
Esccancels placing, puts a carried piece back
Ctrl+Zundo
Wheel · drag empty spacezoom · pan
Relay lettersevery relay gets its own letter (A, B, C ...); a bridge it drives shows the letter in its state colour
JChain: merge / separate. Separate keeps bridges added end to end as two bridges through a Link, so a relay drives just one (a bridge with a relay state always stays separate)
Flex S-bridgejoins two places wherever they are, bending into an S (EXP); any bridge: side panel, Make it a flex S-bridge
Junction on a portplugs straight in, no bridge (EXP); Tab picks its port
